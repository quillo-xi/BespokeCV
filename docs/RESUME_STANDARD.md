# BespokeCV resume standard

This is the design contract for a resume that must survive both machine parsing and fast human review.

## Format

Use a single-column reverse-chronological or combination structure with conventional headings. Keep contact information in the document body. Avoid tables, text boxes, columns, photos, logos, charts, rating bars, decorative icons, and essential information in headers or footers.

Use a common professional font, consistent spacing, simple bullets, and visible hierarchy without breaking linear reading order. One to two pages is a practical default; use the space required to present relevant evidence clearly rather than obeying an arbitrary page target.

## Content hierarchy

1. Name and direct contact information.
2. Recognizable professional headline aligned to the target role where accurate.
3. Concise professional summary focused on value, scope, domain, and differentiation.
4. Focused skills section using recognizable terminology.
5. Reverse-chronological work experience with accomplishment-oriented bullets.
6. Education.
7. Relevant certifications/licenses and optional role-specific sections.

## Accomplishment model

Prefer **action + scope/context + evidence/result**. Strong evidence can be percentage, money, volume, time, quality, risk reduction, geographic or organizational scope, throughput, frequency, team size, portfolio size, or another truthful measure. Not every bullet needs a number; every bullet should demonstrate capability, scope, or impact.

## Tailoring

Use the employer's terminology when it accurately describes the candidate's experience. Spell out uncommon acronyms on first use. Put important skills in experience context, not only in a keyword list. Address required qualifications explicitly when genuinely met.

Never add hidden text, fabricated keywords, inflated titles, nonexistent tools, invented metrics, or credentials not held. An ideal-target resume may be shown only as an explicitly hypothetical reference. Personalized optimized content must be traceable to existing resume evidence or remain visibly annotated as a gap/recommendation.

## File format

Follow the employer's explicit upload instructions first. BespokeCV provides parser-friendly DOCX, print/PDF, and TXT for linearization/parser sanity checking. No format or score can guarantee a particular ATS ranking or hiring decision.
