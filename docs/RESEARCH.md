# Resume research brief — 2026-10-06

BespokeCV is designed for a layered review process: resume parsing and profile creation, search/filtering and skill/qualification matching, recruiter/talent-acquisition review, and hiring-manager review. The objective is not to “beat” one ATS; it is to make truthful qualifications easy to parse, retrieve, compare, and understand.

## Parsing reliability

Greenhouse documents partial/failed parsing risks from graphics and photos, image resumes, complex tables, headers/footers, contact information in text boxes, column layouts, unclear sections, and incomplete or abbreviated job titles. CareerOneStop likewise recommends simple single-column layouts, conventional headings, and avoiding tables, columns, images, and important content in headers or footers.

Design rule: BespokeCV’s ATS master remains one logical column with standard headings and contact information in the document body.

Sources:
- Greenhouse, *Unsuccessful resume parse* (updated 2026-03-02): https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse
- CareerOneStop, *Formatting | Resume Guide*: https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/formatting.aspx

## Modern matching is more than keyword counting

Workday documents resume parsing into candidate fields, machine-learning similarity between skills derived from applications/resumes and requisitions, and greater emphasis on required skills. Workday HiredScore’s Fit & Gap documentation describes qualification-level AI evaluation that points recruiters to supporting evidence.

Design rule: BespokeCV separates **parse integrity**, **target alignment**, **evidence strength**, and **human scan quality** instead of pretending to reproduce a proprietary ATS score.

## Human readability remains distinct

CareerOneStop emphasizes that employers may scan resumes quickly and that the top section should establish role identity and qualifications. A parser-friendly resume still must communicate fit to a person.

Design rule: use a recognizable headline where appropriate, a concise value-oriented summary, focused skills, reverse-chronological experience, and bullets that quickly expose action, scope, and evidence.

Source:
- CareerOneStop, *Top portion of resume*: https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/top-portion-of-resume.aspx

## Accomplishments and evidence

Duty lists describe responsibility; accomplishment bullets demonstrate capability. Quantification is useful where meaningful and truthful, but not every bullet requires a number.

Preferred pattern: **action + scope/context + evidence/result**.

Evidence may include percentage, dollars, time, quality, risk, volume, frequency, sites/regions supported, throughput, reliability, or user/customer impact.

## Length

Current mainstream guidance supports one or two pages depending on relevant experience. A readable two-page resume is preferable to an artificially compressed one-page resume; a padded second page is not.

Sources:
- CareerOneStop, *Formatting | Resume Guide*
- Indeed, *Q&A: When Can a Resume Be Two Pages?* (updated 2026-06-16): https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/when-can-a-resume-be-two-pages

## File format

Advice differs among reputable sources, so employer/application instructions take precedence. Greenhouse and Workday accept multiple common text-based resume formats. BespokeCV maintains DOCX and print/PDF options plus TXT as a linearized sanity check.

## AI hiring and fairness

The EEOC states that employment-discrimination law continues to apply when AI and algorithmic tools are used in hiring and has warned about automated screening that can create unlawful barriers, including for people with disabilities.

Design rule: BespokeCV does not request protected characteristics or use them in its readiness analysis.

Sources:
- EEOC, *Employment Discrimination and AI for Workers*: https://www.eeoc.gov/sites/default/files/2024-04/20240429_Employment%20Discrimination%20and%20AI%20for%20Workers.pdf
- EEOC/DOJ, *Warn against Disability Discrimination*: https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/us-eeoc-and-us-department-justice-warn-against-disability-discrimination

## Submission-ready standard

A strong resume should answer five questions: Can software parse it? Can retrieval/matching systems find the right qualifications? Is there evidence behind the claims? Can a recruiter understand fit quickly? Can a hiring manager defend the decision to interview?

No resume format, keyword density, or score can guarantee passage through every ATS or a hiring decision.
