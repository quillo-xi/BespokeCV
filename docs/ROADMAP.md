# Product roadmap

## 0.1 — Foundation — complete

Local-first responsive builder, ATS-safe master template, target analysis, readiness review, PWA, DOCX/TXT/PDF/JSON exports, documentation, tests, and Pages CI/CD.

## 0.2 — Intake & target sourcing — complete

Document-first resume intake for DOCX/PDF/TXT/backup files, local parsing with source retention, paste/manual fallback, neutral cross-industry helper text, validated job-posting URLs, safe public-page extraction where permitted, and policy-aware fallback for restricted platforms.

## 0.2.1 — Import fidelity hotfix — complete

Multi-page PDF cleanup for repeating page artifacts, wrapped accomplishment bullets, employment duration/location separation, school/program mapping, end-year completion dates, and wrapped skill terms.

## 0.3 — Resume intelligence — complete

Completed in 0.3.0–0.3.8: canonical concept-level target-language extraction, user-curated target concepts, repeatable current/previous job-description evidence intake, required/preferred qualification mapping, Coaching, and resume-grounded evidence examples.

## 0.4 — Review quality & assisted tailoring — in progress

Completed in 0.4.0: multidimensional resume evidence/context model; general Coaching without a target posting; target relevance + evidence-quality bullet ordering; resume-wide date/tense/repetition/duplicate checks; safer rewrite patterns; targeted missing-detail questions; and stronger separation of ATS/parse concerns from human writing quality.

Remaining: higher-fidelity document structure mapping, exact ATS-linearized verification, multiple local resume variants and comparison, accessible section/work-history reordering, and optional AI assistance only through a secure server-side boundary or user-owned provider endpoint.

Also evaluate an optional **user-invoked current-tab capture / clipboard companion** for supported job sites. Any browser companion must use temporary user-granted page access rather than blanket browsing permission, and restricted platforms must remain policy-aware rather than bypassing anti-scraping controls.

## 0.5 — Enterprise capabilities

Only if demand justifies the operational burden: authenticated encrypted sync, team templates, controlled sharing, audit logging, admin policy controls, and organizational deployment.
